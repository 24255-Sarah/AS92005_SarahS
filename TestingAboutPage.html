About Me

Hi, I’m Shana! Welcome to my digital space, where my love for cozy strategy gaming, amateur gardening, and hiking all come together to celebrate Terra Nil. As someone who loves nature and plants, I grew tired of traditional video games that focus on exploiting resources and building concrete cities. Instead, Terra Nil captured my heart by focusing entirely on environmental restoration. While the game doesn't use the explicit terms, the gameplay beautifully mirrors the Māori philosophies of kaitiakitanga (guardianship) and manaakitanga (hospitality and care). It tasks you with healing poisoned soil and preparing a welcoming home for native wildlife. By forcing you to recycle all your technology and fly away at the end, it perfectly reflects my own belief that humans do not own the earth, we are merely temporary caretakers meant to leave it better than we found it.
