How this relates to Kaitiakitanga?

Reverse Building

Traditional city-builders focus on resource extraction and consumption. Terra Nil requires you to purify soil, clean water grids, and reintroduce native wildlife. This reflects the core duty of a kaitiaki (guardian) to heal, sustain, and elevate the health of the natural world.

Leave No Trace

Players must completely recycle and remove all green technology once the ecosystem is self-sustaining. This honors the land by leaving it entirely untouched, ensuring human footprint is temporary and nature is allowed to thrive independently.

Real Impacts

Kaitiakitanga is a physical, active responsibility, not just an abstract concept. By donating game sales to real-world conservation and reforestation efforts, the game bridges virtual actions with tangible protection of the planet.

Play for the Planet

The Mission

Transforming barren wastelands into vibrant, self-sustaining ecosystems. This is the practice of whakaora (restoration). True guardians do not just protect healthy land; they actively heal damaged environments to return them to a state of balance.

How it works?

Using technology to purify water and plant forests, then removing it completely. This demonstrates tiakitanga (care). Humans act only as temporary helpers to kickstart nature, ensuring the land is left to govern itself without ongoing human control or disruption.

Real Impact

This embodies whanaungatanga (interconnectedness). It acknowledges that caring for one small, local patch of land positively impacts the collective health of the entire global environment.
